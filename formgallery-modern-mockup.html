A figure that holds its century.

Each week we open one work to the front of the room and write what it has been doing for the last hundred and fifty years.

001

Kongo Maternity Figure.

Carved sometime between 1845 and 1910 in the Mayombe region of what is now the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Wood, glass, glass beads, brass tacks. Twenty-five centimeters tall and impossibly grave.
